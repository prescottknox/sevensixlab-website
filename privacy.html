Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how SevenSixLab — an independent app studio operated by Raiden Prescott Know (“we”, “us”) — handles information in our mobile applications, including Tango Dance Coach (the “App”).

Short version: we don’t ask you to create an account, we don’t collect personal data, and we don’t sell or share anything about you. Your practice data stays on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or transmit personally identifiable information. The App has no sign-up, no login and no user profiles.

Any content you create while using the App — such as practice settings, progress, favourites and session history — is stored locally on your device. If you have iCloud backup enabled, this data may be included in your own device backup, which is controlled by Apple and by you, not by us.

Device permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, motion data, contacts, location or notifications. It simply runs on your device with no special permissions.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use third-party advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), and we do not build advertising profiles.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example crash reports and download counts) through App Store Connect if you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your iOS settings. This information is aggregated by Apple and cannot be used by us to identify you.

Children’s privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties, because we do not collect it. We may disclose information only if required to do so by law.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you remain in control of it. Deleting the App removes all of its locally stored data from your device. If you contact us by email, we keep that correspondence only for as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export personal data we hold about you, and to object to its processing. Since we do not collect personal data, there is usually nothing for us to hand over — but you are welcome to contact us with any request and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps evolve. The current version will always be published on this page with a new “last updated” date. Material changes will also be noted in the App’s release notes.
