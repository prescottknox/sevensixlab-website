Support

Tango Dance Coach and other SevenSixLab apps

Something not working, a question about how a feature works, or an idea for the next version? Write to us — a real person reads every message and we usually reply within 2 business days.

✉️ support@sevensixlab.com

What to include

To help us fix things faster, please tell us:

  • The app name and version (Settings → About inside the app)
  • Your device and iOS version (e.g. iPhone 15, iOS 18.4)
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if you can

Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use the app?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. Everything works straight away, and your practice data stays on your device.

Is the app free?

Yes. Tango Dance Coach is free — there are no subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Everything in the app is available to you from the moment you install it.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app from your device removes all data it stored. See our privacy policy for details.

I have a feature request

Please send it. Small studios listen closely — a lot of what ships comes straight from user email.

How do I report a bug?

Email us with the details listed above. If the app crashed, please also turn on Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers on your iPhone, so we can see the crash report.

Business enquiries

Press, partnerships or anything else: use the same address, support@sevensixlab.com.
